## Prior art vs. contribution (honest positioning)

- Hu et al. 2025 (2502.18779) already report that without-replacement drafting beats with-replacement drafting and that existing verification algorithms miss the optimum. Our multi-draft results are exact, certified confirmations on small vocabularies, plus a short min-cut proof of opt_wor >= opt_iid, the closed-form cut min_H [2 - p(H) - (1 - q(H))^k] for iid drafts, and the observation that the simple rrs_wor rule often beats the optimal iid coupling.
- Aggarwal & Alman 2022 (2205.06249) give asymptotics of the minimal degree (absolute error, interval [0, B]); our table gives certified exact values for relative error on [-B, B], which is what the polynomial method for attention needs.
- Alman & Song 2023 (2302.13214) and Keles et al. 2022 (2209.04881) give the fine-grained limits; we do not prove new lower bounds. Our rank table quantifies how far the polynomial method is from practical at realistic head dimensions.
- KV-cache: sinks (2309.17453) and heavy hitters (2306.14048) are prior observations on real LLMs; our KV results hold only for an explicit synthetic model.
