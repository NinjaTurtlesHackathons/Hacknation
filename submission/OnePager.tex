{\Large\bfseries Verifier-Gated Discovery Lab}\\[2pt]{\small Agents propose, Omnigent orchestrates, only a code verifier accepts. \quad Commit 26f21a9}\par\medskip
\section{Challenge and users}
Agentic scientific discovery (Databricks challenge). Users: researchers who need results they can trust without re-checking every line.\par
\section{Tools}
Omnigent (orchestration, policies), Claude via the Claude Code CLI, Python verifiers (sympy, python-flint, scipy), MCP server, LaTeX.\par
\section{What worked}
The verifier-gated loop: 3 certified and 1 rejected claims in one run; a surprise reopened an assumption and changed the plan; the family classification moved to 50 / 14 / 24.\par
\section{What was hard}
Making the agents' claims exactly as strong as the evidence; keeping tolerances inside the verifier; parallel agents writing one state file (fixed with a file lock).\par
\section{Time use}
Recorded run 19 min, of which 9 min waiting for human approval; agents/LLM at least 195 s, verifier at most 57 s, experiments at most 196 s.\par
\section{Results}
14 exactly certified violations of the proofreading bound among 88 topologies, 11 of them new in the recorded Omnigent runs; trust benchmark: lab 0.0\% false vs. Claude alone 36.1\%.\par
\section{Measured speedup}
Replay: 4.6x vs. random search (95\% CI 2.6 to 7.5, p = 0.003); vs. no feedback 1.2x (not significant). Result-to-decision latency median 10 s (n = 4). A stopwatch baseline by the team is pending; no comparison with a real laboratory.\par
\section{Next 24 h}
