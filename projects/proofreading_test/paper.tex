\subsection{Abstract}\label{abstract}

An enzyme discriminates a right substrate R from a wrong substrate W
that traverse the same Markov network. W unbinds from bound states
faster by a factor \(e^{\Delta}\). For the linear Hopfield chain with n
= 2 proofreading stages, we certify symbolically that the error rate
satisfies η ≥ \(e^{-3\Delta}\) for all positive rates and all fuel
potentials \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. This makes the
reproduction of Hopfield's bound rigorous
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-neuheit]}. Our main new result concerns networks
with two bound states. The generator produces 92 rule-conforming
topologies, 4 of them degenerate, leaving 88
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-familie]}. Of these, 50 provably obey η ≥
\(e^{-2\Delta}\), 3 have exactly certified counterexamples below it
(fam2\_66, fam2\_9, fam2\_11), and 35 remain open
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}. We also give exactly certified
achievable points of the chain at finite dissipation and speed, and we
report negative results, red-team findings and deviations from the
preregistration.

\subsection{1 Introduction}\label{introduction}

\textbf{Question.} Is the Hopfield-type bound η ≥ \(e^{-(n+1)\Delta}\) a
property of the chain only, or does it hold for general proofreading
networks with the same number of bound states? Hopfield showed that a
simple kinetic pathway, driven strongly but nonspecifically, increases
specificity beyond the equilibrium limit \textsubscript{[C-lit8]}. Later
work addresses error--cost bounds \textsubscript{[C-lit10]}, Pareto
fronts
\textsubscript{[C-lit12]}\textsubscript{[C-lit13]}\textsubscript{[C-lit1]}
and scaling relations \textsubscript{[C-lit22]}. Whether the bound
\(e^{-2\Delta}\) is universal for two bound states is classified here
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}. The classification and its
counterexamples were not found in the retrieved literature
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-neuheit]}.

\textbf{Contribution.} A verifier-gated agentic laboratory produced
exact rational or symbolic certificates. These cover the chain bound,
the achievable points of the chain, and a classification of the
two-bound-state family \textsubscript{[C-methode]}.

\textbf{Summary of results.}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Chain with n = 2: η ≥ \(e^{-3\Delta}\) for all positive rates and fuel
  potentials (Theorem A) \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}.
\item
  Two bound states: the bound \(e^{-2\Delta}\) is not universal
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}. The 88 non-degenerate
  networks split into 50 proved, 3 refuted and 35 open (Theorems B and
  C) \textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}.
\item
  Exact achievable points of the chain at finite σ and v (Theorems D--F)
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10]}.
\item
  The reported kink in the Pareto front of hopfield\_n1 is an optimizer
  artifact
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT2]}.
\item
  Negative results: the claimed necessity of a fuel-driven discard
  feature remains unresolved \textsubscript{[C-neg1]}. The L2c success
  criterion was missed \textsubscript{[C-fakt-praereg]}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{2 Model}\label{model}

The assumptions that carry the results are as follows.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  Both substrates traverse the same Markov network. Every edge has a
  reverse edge. Local detailed balance holds, and cycle affinities arise
  only from the fuel potentials μ per activation and μ\_P per product
  (as stated in the task specification).
\item
  Log-rates lie in {[}-10, 10{]}, so rational rates lie in
  {[}\(e^{-10}\), \(e^{10}\){]} \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1]}.
  Symbolic bounds hold for all positive rates, independent of this range
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}.
\item
  Observables are the error rate η = J\_W/J\_R, the dissipation σ in kT
  per product, and the speed v = J\_R.
\item
  Notation: D = \(e^{\Delta}\) and G = e\^{}μ
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}.
\item
  The family ``bound states ≤ 2'' has one unbound state and an edge
  catalog fixed by the model rules \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R11]}.
  Networks with no possible net production (η = 0/0) are degenerate and
  excluded \textsubscript{[C-fakt-familie]}.
\item
  Admissible edge attributes are constrained. For instance, a
  discriminating edge is allowed only for bound → unbound transitions
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R7-RT2]}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{3 Method: the verifier-gated agentic
lab}\label{method-the-verifier-gated-agentic-lab}

The pipeline has the following components.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Scout.} Literature search over 2752 retrieved sources (arXiv,
  Europe PMC, Crossref, citation chain)
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-recherche]}. 2502 were screened
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-recherche]}. This gave 158 findings whose
  verbatim quote was confirmed by code in the abstract
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-recherche]}. All 11 classic searches had hits
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-recherche]}.
\item
  \textbf{Integrator and preregistration.} Every round was preregistered
  before the experiment (prereg.md) \textsubscript{[C-methode]}.
  Preregistration H6 (commit 6184672) preceded the first run, and
  amendments are dated \textsubscript{[C-fakt-praereg]}.
\item
  \textbf{Researcher agents.} They propose claims and parameters. Their
  interpretations are stored as hypotheses and are never used as results
  (claims with suffix -I).
\item
  \textbf{Code verifier.} It issues two kinds of certificate: (a) exact
  rational reachability, and (b) symbolic positivity proofs (a rational
  function N/D whose coefficients are all nonnegative)
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. It passes its self-test on 18 of
  18 cases (true, false, borderline, rule violations)
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-selbsttest]}.
\item
  \textbf{Red team.} It attacks each claim with a counter-claim. An
  attack may fail, may be inconclusive, or may be non-executable.
\item
  \textbf{Budget.} The lab ran 12 rounds with 11 verified statements and
  1 negative result, at a cost of 3.53 USD \textsubscript{[C-methode]}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{4 Results}\label{results}

``Theorem'' is used only for computed\_rigorous claims. Every claim
below carries this evidence level; none is proved in Lean.

\textbf{Theorem A (Hopfield bound, n = 2; computed\_rigorous).} For
hopfield\_n2, η ≥ 1 * \(e^{-3\Delta}\) for all positive rates and all
fuel potentials μ, μ\_P ≥ 0 \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. The
certificate is a symbolic positivity proof, independent of the rate
range \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. The difference η −
\(e^{-3\Delta}\) is N/D with 1374 and 2048 terms, all coefficients
nonnegative \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. This is a reproduction
of Hopfield's bound, made rigorous for all rates
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-neuheit]}.

\textbf{Theorem B (bound in a subfamily; computed\_rigorous).} For 50
topologies of the family ``bound states ≤ 2'', η ≥ 1/D² for all positive
rates and fuel, where D = \(e^{\Delta}\)
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R11]}. An earlier partial list with 3 of
3 members was proved
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R6]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R7]}.

\textbf{Theorem C (counterexamples; computed\_rigorous).}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  For fam2\_66 there exist rational rates in {[}\(e^{-10}\),
  \(e^{10}\){]} with local detailed balance and η ≤ 0.0001, v ≥ 0.0001
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R4]}. The exact certificate gives η =
  5.082802e-07, σ = 8.341626 kT per product and v = 1.4746e-03
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-fam66]}.
\item
  The edges of fam2\_66 are binding at C0, fuel-driven conversion C0→C1,
  fuel-driven discard from C1, and product formation from C1
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-fam66]}.
\item
  Two further exact counterexamples with η ≤ 0.0001 exist for fam2\_11
  and fam2\_9
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R12]}\textsubscript{[C-fakt-r12]}.
\item
  Consequently the bound \(e^{-2\Delta}\) is not universal for two bound
  states \textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}. The complete
  classification is 50 proved, 3 refuted and 35 open
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}.
\end{itemize}

\emph{Interpretation (not a theorem).} In fam2\_66 the best candidate
mechanism is fuel-driven unbinding from the product-forming state
(editing), which is qualitatively known
\textsubscript{[C-fakt-neuheit]}. Whether this feature is necessary is
unresolved (see the negative results below) \textsubscript{[C-neg1]}.

\textbf{Theorem D (achievable points of hopfield\_n1, I;
computed\_rigorous).} There exist rational rates with local detailed
balance and simultaneously η ≤ 0.0004, σ ≤ 6.0 kT per product and v ≥
0.001 \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1]}. The exact point is η =
3.650169e-04, σ = 6.000000 kT, v = 1.0417e-01
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1]}.

\textbf{Theorem E (achievable points of hopfield\_n1, II;
computed\_rigorous).} The following points are exactly certified
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2]}.

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  η = 3.156120e-04 at σ = 6.000000 kT with v = 2.4255e-02
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2]}.
\item
  η = 2.043712e-04 at σ = 3.079633 kT with v = 2.2592e-03
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2]}.
\end{itemize}

Both are admissible under η ≤ 0.0004 and v ≥ 0.001 with σ\_max = 8
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2]}. The second is also admissible with
σ\_max = 6 \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2-RT2]}.

\textbf{Theorem F (further achievable points, hopfield\_n1;
computed\_rigorous).}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  η = 1.189200e-04 at σ = 4.271813 kT with v = 1.0000e-03, satisfying η
  ≤ 0.0002 and σ ≤ 10 kT \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10]}.
\item
  η = 1.095823e-04 at σ = 0.006292 kT with v = 9.1487e-09, satisfying η
  ≤ 0.00012 and σ ≤ 0.2 kT \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R8]}.
\item
  η = 5.909757e-03 at σ = 3.998555 kT with v = 7.1214e-03, satisfying η
  ≤ 0.01 and v ≥ 0.001 \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R3]}.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{Remark on the Pareto upper curve.} The feasible set grows with
σ\_max, so min η(σ\_max) is non-increasing
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT1]}. The point of Theorem D remains
admissible at σ\_max = 8 and σ\_max = 20, which rules out a jump to η
\textgreater{} 0.008 for σ ≥ 8
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT2]}.
The reported kink, which would parallel the dynamical phase transition
of the mixed regime in the literature \textsubscript{[C-lit2]}, is
therefore an optimizer artifact for hopfield\_n1
\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R1-RT1]}.

\subsection{5 Negative results and red-team
findings}\label{negative-results-and-red-team-findings}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Necessity of a fuel-driven discard (L3).} No claim passed the
  check \textsubscript{[C-neg1]}. The red-team candidates without a
  discard at the product state were not executable (TypeError: Invalid
  NaN comparison) \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R7-RT1]}. A second
  borderline topology violated the model rules (a discriminating edge is
  allowed only for bound → unbound)
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R7-RT2]}.
\item
  \textbf{Failed or non-executable red-team attacks.} Several attacks
  against certified claims did not conclude, because of NaN comparisons
  or index errors
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R4-RT1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R4-RT2]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9-RT1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R11-RT1]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R11-RT2]}\textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R12-RT2]}.
  These are failures of the attack, not logical contradictions.
\item
  \textbf{Attacks that did not prove their counter-claim.}

  \begin{itemize}
  \tightlist
  \item
    The symbolic attack that η ≥ 1.2·\(e^{-2\Delta}\) holds for all
    rates failed (coefficients not all nonnegative), as expected given
    the certified point of Theorem F
    \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R8-RT1]}. The attacker's own search
    found η\_min = 1.0962e-04 at σ = 0.090, v = 6.296e-08, against a
    claimed 1.2500e-04 \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R8-RT2]}.
  \item
    For n = 2, the attacker's search found η\_min = 3.1387e-05 at σ =
    2665.585, v = 2.037e-06, and did not reach the claimed 1.0000e-07
    \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9-RT2]}.
  \item
    Attacks on fam2\_9, fam2\_11 and fam2\_13 found the bound unprovable
    for them, consistent with counterexamples
    \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R12-RT1]}.
  \end{itemize}
\item
  \textbf{Tighter bound for hopfield\_n1.} The σ = 6 optimum (η ≈
  3.16e-4) does not meet the tighter bound η\_max = 2.2e-4 at σ\_max = 8
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2-RT1]}. The point with η =
  2.043712e-04 is nonetheless certified separately
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R2-RT2]}.
\item
  \textbf{Weak-driving check.} The attacker's search at the claimed
  parameter regime found η\_min = 3.3490e-03 at σ = 0.422, v = 2.719e-03
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10-RT2]}. The claimed value 2.0000e-03
  was not matched \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10-RT2]}. The symbolic
  bound check was inconclusive \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10-RT1]}.
  The exact certificate of the corresponding achievable point stands
  independently \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R10]}.
\item
  \textbf{Saturation of the n = 1 front.} The symbolic attack on the
  saturation floor was not certified (symbolic computation
  \textgreater{} 600 s) \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R3-RT2]}. The
  question of saturation toward the Hopfield limit is therefore not
  answered here.
\item
  \textbf{Preregistration deviations.} The L2c success criterion (at
  least 5 counterexamples in a round) was missed: 2 in round 12, 3 in
  total \textsubscript{[C-fakt-praereg]}. Degenerate networks (η = 0/0)
  were excluded \textsubscript{[C-fakt-familie]}.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{6 Limitations and open
questions}\label{limitations-and-open-questions}

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  35 of the 88 topologies with two bound states are open: neither a
  proof of η ≥ \(e^{-2\Delta}\) nor an exact counterexample is available
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-klassifikation]}.
\item
  The mechanism behind fam2\_66 is an interpretation. Its necessity is
  unresolved \textsubscript{[C-neg1]}.
\item
  Within the claim list, only n = 2 carries a certified chain bound
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R9]}. Corresponding certificates for
  other n are not part of the retained claims.
\item
  The certified achievable points are single points, not complete Pareto
  fronts. Saturation toward the Hopfield limit is not established
  \textsubscript{[C-proofreading-R3-RT2]}.
\item
  Novelty: the classification and counterexamples were not found in the
  retrieved literature, but that search is limited to the 158 confirmed
  findings
  \textsubscript{[C-fakt-neuheit]}\textsubscript{[C-fakt-recherche]}.
\end{itemize}
