What the theorem does and does not prove
We prove G(31) ≥ 81 and G(41) ≥ 111 by finite exact witnesses. We do not prove these sets globally maximal, refute the triangular-lattice conjecture, or claim that distance-palette exchange is a new method. Later-literature novelty is separately audited in the paper.
