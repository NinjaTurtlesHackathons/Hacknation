# Published conjecture scope checked before search

Primary source: [Bao–Yu, Constructions of Large m-Distance Sets on Triangular Lattice, Conjecture 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.00880).

The conjecture concerns each filled regular hexagon and each filled equiangular hexagon whose side lengths alternate consecutive integers: the configuration should be maximum for its own distance-class count. It does not assert that every optimal construction has one of those shapes; the paper separately discusses deletion-based configurations.

Accordingly, more than 91 points with at most 34 distances, or more than 75 with at most 28, would contradict the corresponding published full-hexagon cases. A 111-point/41-distance witness does not directly contradict those cases. The paper's table explicitly treats at most m distances and repeats an 11-distance configuration for m=12; exact attained counts nevertheless avoid convention ambiguity.
