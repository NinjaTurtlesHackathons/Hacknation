# Adversarial novelty review: 81/31 and 111/41

Audit date: 2026-10-04. Reviewer: packing_scout, separately assigned literature critic. This review does not infer novelty from a successful verifier.

## Verdict and permitted wording

The exact existence claims **G(31) >= 81** and **G(41) >= 111**, where G counts at most k nonzero Euclidean distances, survive this review. They improve the explicit Ahmed–Snevily 2013 published lower bounds 80 and 109, respectively. No equal or stronger witness was located in the primary sources and author code audited below. Recommended wording: “Two exact constructions improve the lower bounds in Ahmed–Snevily (2013); our literature audit through 4 October 2026 found no prior equal or stronger construction.” This is a qualified literature conclusion, not proof that no unpublished or unindexed construction exists. Do not claim exact G values, global optimality, or an extraordinary general mechanism solely from these two gains (1.25% and approximately1.835%).

The 111-point witness also yields the at-most-40 comparison only if it has <=40 distances; it has41, so no such claim. At k42 the published115 construction remains stronger. Monotonicity gives111 at41, but cannot reverse direction.

## Implicit-family attack and unresolved objections

The already existing general bladed-array language is broad. The available necessary Pa-core/unit-triangle test (A,r1 up to15, all allowedr2, six rotations and translations) found zero matches for111, as previously for81; result bladed111.json. This rejects that precise one-layer implementation only. It is NOT a proof against every interpretation of arbitrarily added triangular blades, nor against a multi-layer or enlarged published construction. Never say that the candidates refute the 2013 optimal-bladed-array question without a rigorous universal bladed bound.

Bao’s conjectures concern **maximum** sets. Finding a larger witness than one table row cannot refute a conjecture about the structure of global maximizers. Their regular/alternating hexagon conjecture also applies at those hexagons’ own distance counts: k31/41 do not automatically provide counterexamples. Likewise, a diameter-limited C6-symmetry exclusion cannot rule out symmetric constructions with a larger diameter or another similarity scaling.
