# Prospectively registered research protocol

2026-10-04. Commit before first discovery search. This is constructive geometry, not a statistical benchmark. No 10x claim is inferred from runtime.

Primary criterion: rational/algebraic witness improving the strongest accessible same-normalization published construction by a margin exceeding source rounding; separately audit novelty. Exact theorem requires a complete proof and independent attack.

Search methods permitted: multistart epigraph optimization; sequential LP with exact feasibility re-evaluation; boundary/contact class enumeration; point deletion/insertion; cross-container affine transfer; graph/family parameterization; symmetry release; rational reconstruction. Seeds 1000–1019 for first multistart batches; further seed ranges must be logged before their batch. All trials retained, including failures.

Verifier criterion: at least two positive examples, two false examples and one rule-violation rejection, then exact full enumeration. Agents cannot provide tolerance or alter normalization. New verifier flaws trigger a regression case before further search.

Escalation: compare against recent author data, not old rounded tables. Numerical micro-improvements below 0.05% are infrastructure/precision findings unless an exact substantive theorem follows. Stagnation after a batch triggers a different hypothesis or target; more identical restarts alone is not escalation. Paper must lead with strongest accepted claim, and explicitly state when no new discovery has been demonstrated.
